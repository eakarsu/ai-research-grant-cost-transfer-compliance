# Research Grant Cost-Transfer Compliance

Prevent unsupported cost transfers and preserve reimbursable research expenditures through evidence, timing, and allocation controls.

**Primary buyer:** Universities and research institutions. **Evidence:** award budgets, general-ledger transactions, payroll, effort, invoices, transfer requests, allocability evidence, approvals, deadlines, and sponsor reports.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Award budget registry
- Ledger transaction ingestion
- Payroll effort linkage
- Original charge validation
- Transfer reason classification
- Allocability assessment
- Benefit-to-award evidence
- Ninety-day deadline control
- Cost share treatment
- PI approval workflow
- High-risk transfer review
- Journal entry generation
- Sponsor reporting reconciliation
- Audit evidence package
- Department award analytics
